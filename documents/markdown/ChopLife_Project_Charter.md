## 1. Project Overview

ChopLife is a mobile food delivery platform designed specifically for the African market. The platform connects busy professionals and 9-5 workers to quality local and exotic restaurants, enabling them to browse menus, place orders, make secure payments, and track their deliveries — all from one seamless, intuitive app.

The African food delivery space is growing rapidly, but existing solutions consistently fail to address the realities of the local market: unreliable delivery infrastructure, limited local restaurant options, expensive delivery fees, and platforms that are not built around how Africans actually use mobile technology. ChopLife is purpose-built to close that gap.

ChopLife will launch simultaneously across three countries — Nigeria, Ghana, and Cameroon — with a physical office presence in each country to support local vendor onboarding, rider management, customer support, and regulatory compliance.

## 2. Project Goal

The primary goal of ChopLife is to become the most trusted and widely used food delivery platform across West and Central Africa by delivering a product that is fast, affordable, intelligent, and deeply rooted in the African dining experience.

Specific goals include:

- Build and launch a fully functional mobile food delivery application on both iOS and Android platforms within 12 months

- Establish operational offices in Nigeria, Ghana, and Cameroon to support local market needs

- Onboard a minimum of 50 restaurant vendors per country within the first 3 months of launch

- Achieve a minimum of 5,000 active users within 6 months of launch

- Process at least 500 successful orders per day within the first quarter of operations

- Maintain an average app rating of 4.2 stars or above across all platforms

## 3. Problem Statement

Across major African cities, busy professionals and 9-5 workers face a persistent problem: accessing quality, affordable meals during work hours and after a long day without the hassle of going out. Existing food delivery platforms operating in Africa either:

- Lack sufficient coverage of local and culturally relevant restaurant options

- Charge high delivery fees that make regular use unaffordable

- Offer a poor mobile experience that does not account for lower-bandwidth environments

- Fail to support diverse local payment methods such as mobile money

- Provide no intelligent personalisation to help users discover new meals they will love

ChopLife directly addresses each of these pain points with a product built from the ground up for the African context.

## 4. Target Users

ChopLife is designed primarily for:

**Primary Users**

- Busy professionals and 9-5 workers aged 22–45 in urban areas across Nigeria, Ghana, and Cameroon who want quality meals delivered to their offices or homes without disrupting their work schedule

- Individuals and households looking for convenient, affordable food options from a wide variety of local and exotic restaurants

- Groups of colleagues or friends who want to place joint orders and split the cost effortlessly

**Secondary Users**

- Restaurant and food vendors seeking a reliable digital channel to reach more customers and grow their businesses

- Delivery riders looking for a structured, app-managed platform to earn income consistently

- Small and medium food businesses with no existing digital presence who want to get online quickly

## 5. ChopLife Product & Services

ChopLife is more than just an app — it is an end-to-end food delivery ecosystem. The platform consists of three interconnected products:

### 5.1 Customer Mobile App (iOS & Android)

- User Authentication & Profile Management

- Menu & Food Browsing with search and filter

- Cart, Order Summary & Checkout

- Payment Gateway — card, mobile money, and cash on delivery

- Real-time Order Tracking

- AI-Powered Meal Suggestions based on cravings and budget

- Group Ordering with Bill Splitting

- Ratings & Reviews

- Push Notifications

- Customer Support / Help Center

- Inter-State Ordering

### 5.2 Vendor/Restaurant Dashboard

- Restaurant profile and menu management

- Order management and fulfilment tracking

- Sales analytics and performance reports

- AI-driven visibility boost based on ratings

### 5.3 Rider Management Platform

- Delivery rider registration and onboarding

- Automated order assignment and dispatch

- Live rider tracking for customers and operations

- Earnings dashboard for riders

### 5.4 Physical Office Presence

ChopLife will maintain a dedicated physical office in each of its three launch countries to support:

- Nigeria Office — Lagos (Head Office): National operations, engineering team, investor relations

- Ghana Office — Accra: Local vendor onboarding, customer support, and marketing

- Cameroon Office — Douala: French/English bilingual support, local compliance and partnerships

Each office will be staffed with a Country Manager, vendor relations officers, customer support agents, and a logistics coordinator to ensure smooth local operations.

## 8. Project Methodology

ChopLife will be delivered using a Hybrid Project Management methodology, combining the structured planning rigour of Waterfall with the flexibility and iterative delivery of Agile/Scrum.

**Why Hybrid?**

The nature of ChopLife requires both approaches. The foundational phases — requirements gathering, architecture design, office setup, and compliance — benefit from Waterfall's sequential, well-documented structure. The product development and testing phases benefit from Agile's sprint-based iterations, which allow the team to build, test, and refine features continuously based on user feedback.

|                       |              |                                                                       |
|-----------------------|--------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Phase**             | **Approach** | **Rationale**                                                         |
| Initiation & Planning | Waterfall    | Structured documentation, charter, and stakeholder alignment          |
| Architecture & Design | Waterfall    | System design requires sequential decisions before development begins |
| Feature Development   | Agile/Scrum  | 2-week sprints allow iterative feature delivery and early feedback    |
| Testing & QA          | Agile/Scrum  | Continuous testing embedded in each sprint                            |
| Launch & Deployment   | Waterfall    | Structured go-live checklist and staged country rollout               |

**Scrum Ceremonies**

- Sprint Planning — every 2 weeks at the start of each sprint

- Daily Standups — 15-minute check-ins to surface blockers

- Sprint Reviews — demo of completed features to the sponsor

- Sprint Retrospectives — team reflection and process improvement

- Backlog Grooming — weekly refinement of the product backlog
